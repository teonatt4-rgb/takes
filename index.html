I trained as a classical violinist. Later I found my way into jazz, where you rarely hear the violin. TAKES is a record of how I have been turning a borrowed language into my own voice. It runs from the first standard I ever picked up to a piece I wrote myself. Choose a record to begin. 我是古典出身的小提琴手，后来一头扎进了爵士——一件你很少在爵士里听到的乐器。TAKES 不是一份整齐的曲目表，而是一个演奏者如何把"借来的语言"变成"自己的声音"的记录：从我上手的第一首标准曲，到一首完全属于自己的作品。选一张唱片，开始。

Approach自述

I was trained to play a score faithfully. Jazz taught me to ask a different question: how do I make a piece my own?

I come from strings, the European canon and the discipline of notation. Jazz came later. In jazz the violin is an outsider, and I want to explore that distance. After four years of listening, transcribing and making choices, I can no longer trace where each phrase began. I think that is the moment a borrowed language starts to become a voice.

To me, improvising and arranging are a human way of creating music. Today they stand beside machine generation. They can be in dialogue with it, and they are not replaced by it. That question runs through everything here. It becomes the main subject of my original work, Dream of Martian Consciousness. The classical canon is only one part of music. Jazz, and the music still being written today, leave so much to explore. That is the work I want to keep doing.

我受的训练，是忠实地呈现一份乐谱；而爵士教会我问另一个问题——不是"如何演奏一首曲子"，而是"如何让它成为我的"。弦乐、欧洲经典、记谱的纪律，是我的来处；爵士来得更晚，小提琴在其中是个外来者，而这段距离正是我想探索的。四年的聆听、扒谱与抉择之后，我已记不清每个乐句的起点——而那恰恰是"借来的语言"开始成为"声音"的时刻。我把即兴与编配视为一种"人的生成系统"，它与今天的机器生成并置、对话，而非被取代。这个命题贯穿这里的一切，并在我的原创作品《梦的火星意识》中成为明确的主题。古典并非音乐的全部；爵士，以及此刻仍在被书写的音乐，还有那么多值得探索的地方。这就是我想一直做下去的事。

Classical music is where I began. Jazz is where I keep asking questions. This is the voice I am still building, one record at a time. 古典是我的起点，爵士是我不断发问的地方。这是我仍在塑造的声音——一张唱片，一张唱片地。

— Liang Juetong 梁珏童
